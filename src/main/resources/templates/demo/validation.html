Dynamic Validation

The form binds straight to a Constrained bean whose rules live on the bean itself. Submit it and RIFE2 validates every field server-side, returning a precise error for each one that fails.

The constraints don't have to live on the bean. With metadata merging you keep the bean a plain POJO and declare the same rules in a companion SignupMetaData class that RIFE2 weaves in.

The rules are declared once on the bean and split into named groups. Validate everything, or just one group, like a single step of a wizard. Try an empty field, a bad email, or an age like 5.
